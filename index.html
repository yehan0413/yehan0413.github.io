Han Ye 叶晗

Assistant Professor, Department of Economics

I am an Assistant Professor at the Department of Economics of Lingnan University. I received my PhD in Economics from the National University of Singapore.

My research interests center on health economics, behavioral economics, labor economics, and applied microeconometrics.
